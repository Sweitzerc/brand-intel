How to read this
  • Census origin is the country where goods were made or mined, not the country they shipped from.
  • Census suppresses small cells. A missing value at a small port is not a zero.
  • AIS is coastal-only (land receivers). Calls count all cargo and tanker ships at the port, not only ships carrying these goods.
  • Guam is outside the Census customs territory and has no trade figures.
  • HHI (0–10,000): under 1,500 unconcentrated, 1,500–2,500 moderately concentrated, over 2,500 highly concentrated.
  • The vessel section is an allocation model. No source here records which ship carried which goods.
